# Bobcat Course Match 🐾

A quick quiz that helps Texas State students find free Coursera courses that fit them.

**Try it:** https://sergio-i1.github.io/bobcat-course-match/

## What it does

1. Students answer a 3-minute quiz about their skills and interests.
2. They see a score for six skills: leadership, teamwork, communication, hands-on tech, data, and planning.
3. They get Coursera courses picked for them, plus hands-on options on campus (like the Alkek One badges).
4. They can browse every course by topic, skill, hours, and online or in person, and save their top picks as a PDF.

## Why

Texas State students get Coursera Career Academy for free, but many don't know it exists, and the ones who do often get lost in thousands of courses. This site makes the first step easy.

## Notes

- The whole site is one file: `index.html`. The course list is in one block near the top of the code, so it's easy to edit.
- Optional anonymous visit counter (GoatCounter): set `GOATCOUNTER_CODE` in `index.html`. It doesn't use cookies or store IP addresses.
- Course hours are rough estimates. Check each course page.
- Student-built prototype, made with the help of AI. Not an official Texas State or Coursera site.
